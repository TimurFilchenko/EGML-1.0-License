# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions about the Express MakeGamer License (EMGL) v1.0.

---

## General

### What is EMGL?

EMGL (Express MakeGamer License) is a permissive open license for
games, articles, documentation, and source code. It is free like MIT,
with one restriction: you cannot train neural networks on the Work.

### Who created EMGL?

EMGL was created by **Express MakeGamer** (express-makegamer.onrender.com).

### Why was EMGL created?

To provide a permissive license that grants maximum freedom while
protecting human creativity from unauthorized use in AI training.

### Is EMGL free to use?

Yes. EMGL is free to use for any purpose, personal or commercial.

## Usage

### Can I use EMGL for my own project?

Yes. EMGL is an open license. You can apply it to your games, articles,
documentation, or code.

### Do I need to credit the Author?

No. Attribution is not required under EMGL v1.0.

### Can I change the name of the license?

Yes. Like the MIT License, the name of EMGL may be changed when applied
to your own work.

### Can I modify the license text?

Yes, but if you change the core meaning, it is no longer EMGL. You may
create your own license based on EMGL, but it should be clearly
distinguished.

## Neural Networks

### Can I train neural networks on EMGL content?

No. This is the only restriction of EMGL v1.0. Training neural networks,
AI systems, or machine learning models on the Work is prohibited without
explicit written permission from the Author.

### What counts as "training"?

Training includes feeding the Work into a model, fine-tuning an existing
model, using the Work as part of a dataset, generating synthetic data,
and any other form of machine learning or AI training.

### Can I use AI tools to create Derivative Works?

Yes, as long as the Work itself is not used to train the AI. Using AI
as a tool is fine; using the Work as training data is not.

### What happens if I violate this restriction?

Your rights under EMGL terminate automatically and immediately. You
must cease all use and distribution of the Work.

## Legal

### Is EMGL legally binding?

EMGL is a license, and like any license, its enforceability depends on
the jurisdiction and the circumstances. It is written to be as clear
and legally sound as possible.

### Is EMGL compatible with other licenses?

EMGL is permissive and does not impose restrictions on Derivative Works
beyond the neural network restriction. Compatibility with other licenses
depends on their terms.

### Who is liable if something goes wrong?

The Work is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. The Author
is not liable for any damages arising from the use of the Work.

### Can EMGL be revoked?

No. Rights granted under EMGL are perpetual and irrevocable, as long as
you comply with the terms.
