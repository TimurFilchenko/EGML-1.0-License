## Overview

The Express MakeGamer License (EMGL) is a permissive open license
created for developers, authors, and creators who believe in freedom
of use, modification, and distribution, while preserving the principle
of protecting human creativity from unauthorized use in artificial
intelligence training.

EMGL is designed to be simple, readable, and legally sound. It grants
maximum freedom to anyone who uses it, with one clearly defined
restriction.

## What You Cannot Do

The only restriction of EMGL v1.0:

- **You cannot train neural networks** on the Work, in whole or in

This includes training new models, fine-tuning existing models, using
the Work as part of a dataset, and any other form of AI or machine
learning training.
